Enhance Your Cleaning Experience

Customize Your Clean

Every home has unique needs. At Clean By Lesa LLC, we offer customizable cleaning enhancements to give your space the extra attention it deserves. Whether you’re looking for detailed appliance cleaning, refreshed surfaces, or help with organizing, our additional services can be tailored to your needs.

❄

Interior Refrigerator Cleaning

Refresh your refrigerator with a detailed cleaning of interior shelves, drawers, and accessible surfaces to remove spills, residue, and buildup.

♨

Interior Oven Cleaning

Remove grease, food residue, and baked-on buildup from the inside of your oven for a cleaner, fresher cooking space.

◇

Interior Cabinet Cleaning

Detailed cleaning of the inside of kitchen or bathroom cabinets and drawers to remove dust, crumbs, and accumulated debris.

▧

Interior Window Cleaning

Brighten your space with detailed cleaning of accessible interior window glass, removing fingerprints, smudges, and everyday buildup.

✧

Baseboard Cleaning

Restore the appearance of your baseboards with detailed dusting and wiping to remove dirt, scuff marks, and accumulated buildup.

⌁

Wall Washing

Refresh interior walls with careful spot cleaning or washing of suitable surfaces to address fingerprints, marks, and everyday dirt.

▤

Blind Cleaning

Remove accumulated dust and surface buildup from accessible blinds for a cleaner, more refreshed appearance.

▱

Linen Change

Enjoy freshly made beds with our convenient linen-changing service using clean linens provided by the client.

♡

Pet Hair Removal

Target stubborn pet hair on furniture, upholstery, and other suitable surfaces for a cleaner, more comfortable home.

♨

Steam Cleaning

Enhance your cleaning with high-temperature steam treatment of suitable surfaces to help loosen stubborn dirt, grease, and buildup.

✚

Disinfection & Sanitizing

Additional attention to frequently touched surfaces using appropriate disinfecting and sanitizing products to support a cleaner environment.

▦

Decluttering & Organizing

Bring order back to your space with personalized assistance sorting, arranging, and organizing belongings to create a more functional home.

✦

Reorganization

Reorganize belongings and rooms with personalized support to improve everyday function, flow, and comfort.

Important Information

Additional services are available upon request and may involve an additional charge based on the size, condition, and scope of work.

For interior refrigerator, oven, and cabinet cleaning, clients are asked to remove personal belongings and contents before service. Removal and replacement of items by our cleaning team may be arranged for an additional fee.
